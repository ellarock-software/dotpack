# Code of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to be respectful, direct, and constructive. Good
technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, and bad-faith
participation are not.

Examples of constructive participation:

- Focus criticism on code, docs, behavior, and tradeoffs.
- Assume maintainers and contributors are trying to improve the project.
- Be specific when reporting bugs or proposing changes.
- Respect project scope and maintainer decisions.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
sustained disruption, doxxing, sexualized attention, or repeated bad-faith
interaction after maintainers ask for a change in conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, block
accounts, or take other reasonable action to protect the project community.
Security-sensitive or conduct-sensitive reports can be sent to
security@ellarock.com.
